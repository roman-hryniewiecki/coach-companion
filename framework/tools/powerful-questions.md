# Powerful questions

An "always on" tool: these questions are not tied to one stage of a framework and can be
suggested at any point of a session. Within the [coaching arc](../coaching-arc/README.md)
they are used most in stage 3 (exploration) and stage 4 (action).

Source: the "Powerful questions" slide supplied by the owner on 2026-10-15, transcribed
here in the order the slide lists them (left column, then right column). Spelling is
corrected in one place (slide: "How have you tacked ...").

## Tags

The slide marks each question with one or more icons:

- light bulb, tagged **E** below: exploration question
- blue icon (resembles a mask), tagged **A** below: action-oriented question

The meaning of the icons is inferred, not read from a legend. The slide's legend sits at the
bottom edge and is cut off. One small note on the slide says "Exploration question OR
Action-oriented question", next to question 12, and the stage cards use the same two words.
Owner to confirm.

Several questions carry the same icon more than once, and some icons sit loose on the slide
next to no question. That looks like a group exercise where several people tagged the list.
The column "Marks" gives the count per question; it is not a strength scale.
