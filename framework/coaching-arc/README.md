# Coaching Arc

The first framework Coach Companion implements. It is the scaffold for a whole coaching
conversation: five stages, walked through in order from the opening agreement to the close.
The [powerful questions](../tools/powerful-questions.md) are not part of any one stage; they
are "always on" and can be suggested at any point of the arc.

Source: two slides supplied by the owner on 2026-10-15 (the arc diagram and the five stage
cards), transcribed here. The slide images themselves are not stored in the repository.

## The arc at a glance

| # | Id | Arc label | Card title | Icons on the arc slide |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `align` | Align Expectations | Alliance or Working Agreement | checklist on a clipboard |
| 2 | `goal` | Goal Identification | Growth Goal Identification | orange target (a gears-in-a-speech-bubble icon sits between stages 1 and 2) |
| 3 | `explore` | Exploration & Learning | Exploration for learning | magnifying glass, light bulb, gold nugget |
| 4 | `action` | Establish Success | Action and Forwarding learning | ladder, target with an arrow in the centre |
| 5 | `growth` | Facilitate growth | Facilitate growth | graduation cap |

The arc slide and the card slide use different names for stages 1, 2, 3 and 4; both are kept
above. The ids are the names the code will use. The arc rises from stage 1 to the top at
stage 3 (Exploration & Learning is the widest segment, at the apex) and comes down again
through stages 4 and 5.

## Stages

### 1. Align Expectations: Alliance or Working Agreement

- Expectations for the conversation
- Confidentiality
- Anything else to add/change

### 2. Goal Identification: Growth Goal Identification

- What is needed to discuss?
- What is the opportunity for growth for the client?
- What is behind the story they bring in?
- Define a clear, measurable goal for the session

### 3. Exploration & Learning: Exploration for learning

- Search for insights and learning
- Powerful questions
- Focus on the person that wants to grow
- The client is the expert
- Learning and growth reveal themselves in "golden nuggets"
- Explorative Questions

The last bullet sits on the bottom edge of the card in the source slide; there may have been
more text below it that was cut off.

### 4. Establish Success: Action and Forwarding learning

- Help the client turn the new awareness and insight into a practical action step
- Clear obstacles and reveal resources
- Accountability on the client

### 5. Facilitate growth

- Learning from the learning
- What is different for the client now?
- Close on the client's terms

## Links to the powerful questions

The question list tags each question as an exploration question, an action-oriented
question, or both (see [powerful-questions.md](../tools/powerful-questions.md)). Exploration
questions fit stage 3, action-oriented questions fit stage 4. This pairing is an inference
from the stage names and the legend; the slides do not state it.
